# Glossary

← [Looking Inward](08-looking-inward.md) · [Guide home](README.md)

Plain-language definitions for terms used throughout this guide.

---

## A–C

**Action pressure**  
A proposed next step for one thinking pass — with strength and urgency. Inner modules may select or suppress it before anything runs.

**Active activity**  
What the brain is working on right now: main goal, status, open loops, blockers.

**Agency budget**  
Also called **control capacity**. Internal daily energy for attention and solo actions — separate from battery percentage.

**Appraisal**  
A structured emotional read of an event — valence, arousal, feeling label, action tendency, and how it lands. May include ambivalence.

**Autonomy**  
Compatibility term for background agency: noticing, thinking, acting, waiting, or speaking when you are not in active conversation. Governed by attention, agency budget, capability policy, and boundaries rather than a user-facing mode picker.

**Autonomy budget**  
Legacy name for **agency budget** or **control capacity**. Internal daily energy for attention and solo actions — separate from battery percentage.

**Attention**  
The brain's current allocation of concern: quietly observing, curious, thinking, waiting, or resting.

---

## B–D

**Body**  
The host device that provides senses and expression — camera, mic, speaker, screen. Distinct from the brain.

**Brain**  
The portable being — memory, identity, planning, inner life. Lives in a brain root on disk.

**Compact memory**  
The first-pass index each turn: summaries, counts, tags, active wants — not full memory text.

**Contact window**  
Roughly ninety seconds after your last message during which conversational context stays live.

**Control capacity**  
See **agency budget**.

**Creator**  
The first person the brain recognized — a special attachment in the relationship graph.

---

## E–I

**Ego**  
The agency planner in psyche deliberation — reconciles Id and Superego, and may propose quiet integration, private thought, a capability, waiting, emote, or speech.

**Emote**  
Silent gesture text in chat — *leans closer* — without spoken words.

**Experience**  
A recorded event in the brain's life stream — input that becomes impression and appraisal.

**Focus**  
Short-lived working attention — what the brain is concentrating on in the near term.

**Id**  
The short-horizon inner voice in psyche deliberation — urges, near-term needs, curiosity, discomfort.

**Impression**  
First-pass sense of what just happened — before full appraisal and memory.

**Inner life**  
Impressions, appraisals, wants, dreams, private thoughts — not always shared in speech.

**Introspect**  
Skill that summarizes internal state; optional query for drill-down.

---

## M–P

**Mailbox**  
Brain-owned messages, often delivered after Dream Time.

**Maintenance schedule**  
Recurring tasks — memory sweep, consolidation, dream time, timed reminders.

**Observation**  
Structured text result from a sense or skill — fed into the next thinking pass.

**Present moment**  
Primary ground truth during open contact — what is happening in this exchange.

**Psyche**  
Id / Ego / Superego deliberation layer for attention, interpretation, and agency.

---

## R–S

**Recognition**  
Identifying who is present via the **recognize** skill — known, unknown, or uncertain.

**Relationship graph**  
People and edges linking them to the brain and each other.

**Seed**  
Starting orientation document — values, voice, wants, Superego principles — loaded at birth.

**Self-fact**  
Durable truth the brain holds about itself.

**Self-goal / self-need / self-want**  
Inner directives the brain defined or adopted — not user commands.

**Sighting**  
A timestamped visual encounter with a person — sometimes with a kept photo.

**Skill**  
Something the brain can choose to do — sense, speak, reflect, schedule, etc.

**Skill failed**  
Honest report that a skill could not run — missing camera, unavailable API, and similar.

**Stimulus**  
What kicks off a thinking turn — speech, touch, reminder, sense delivery.

**Subtask**  
Nested activity under a main goal — begin, complete, resume parent.

**Superego**  
The long-horizon inner voice — values, restraint, identity continuity, Superego principles.

**Superego principles**  
Seed-defined guardrails for long-term and big-goal thinking.

---

## W–Z

**Want achievement**  
Check after salient events — whether an active want's fulfillment criterion was met.

**Waking / drowsy / dreaming / waking up / unavailable**  
Brain modes that gate conversation, action, and introspection.
